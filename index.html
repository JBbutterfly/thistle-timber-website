What we do

Three pillars, one mission

Thistle & Timber Foundation: creating space for wonder and connection with the natural world — restoring native prairies, protecting and enhancing woodlands, and inviting children and adults to reconnect with nature.

Why a night hike

The prairie and woodland at night are a completely different experience — sound, movement, the insects and animals that only come out after dark. It's one of the most direct ways to feel what restoration is protecting.
