For Funders & Institutional Partners

Grants & institutional support

Thistle & Timber Foundation, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) public charity under section 170(b)(1)(A)(vi) — EIN 39-5011326, Indiana ID #2961860.

What we bring

  • An active pilot restoration plot at Beall Heritage Reserve for field observation
  • A documented, replicable herbicide-free methodology (Xerces Society-based protocols)
  • 501(c)(3) nonprofit status — grant-eligible
  • A growing community engagement and volunteer network
  • Landowner perspective and long-term commitment to the land

What we're seeking

  • Research partnerships and peer review of our methods
  • Soil health and plant establishment data support
  • Access to native-plant and restoration research networks
  • Educational programming collaboration for youth
  • Letters of support for grant applications
