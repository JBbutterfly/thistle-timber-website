Volunteer Workdays

Hands-on restoration, one session at a time

This is where Connection meets Restoration directly: show up, and we'll show you what to pull and where. No experience needed.

How it works

  • Guided invasive-removal sessions — garlic mustard, buckthorn, honeysuckle, reed canarygrass
  • Open to adults and youth groups
  • Scheduled seasonally, spring and fall
Sign up for a work session

Age & consent policy

  • Volunteers 13+ may log their own hours
  • Youth 13–17 need a parental consent form on file (collected in person)
  • Children under 13 participate alongside a parent or guardian — their time is credited to the parent, because connecting kids to nature is part of our mission
After a work session

Log your hours at tally.so/r/jaqeY4 (quick, QR-friendly). Donating specialized services, equipment use, or goods instead? Reach out to us directly and we'll make sure it's logged. Every hour matters for grant reporting and credibility — general volunteer time is valued at the Independent Sector rate, updated annually.

Families, homeschool groups, and youth organizations from Porter County and beyond — our seasonal community events and volunteer workdays are open to all ages, with children under 13 always alongside a parent or guardian.
