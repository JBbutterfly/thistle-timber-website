Our Story

It started with a box of caterpillars.

Watching children witness a caterpillar's transformation first-hand, our founder kept coming back to one question: why am I not doing more? Years of watching herbicides sprayed across rights-of-way that could have been habitat made the gap impossible to ignore — between what we're doing to the land, and what's possible for it.

Mission
Thistle & Timber Foundation: creating space for wonder and connection with the natural world — restoring native prairies, protecting and enhancing woodlands, and inviting children and adults to reconnect with nature.

We're a young foundation, founded in 2026 by people who are, in large part, close friends and family to each other first. That's on purpose — the belief that carries this work is as much about the people around the table as it is about the acres. Our founder's two children were the original reason to start: the hope is that they, and every other kid who comes to the Reserve, gets to grow up knowing what wild actually feels like.

The Three Pillars

What we believe, in three parts

The mission's three clauses map one-to-one onto three pillars — the only place all three live together, in full.

Restoration & Stewardship

We heal land the slow, honest way: herbicide-free, hands-on, and built to last.

Biodiversity & Habitat

Every acre restored is a home returned — to monarchs, fireflies, and the species that belong here.

Connection & Wonder

Wonder is the on-ramp: we invite children and adults to fall back in love with the natural world.
