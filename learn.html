Connection & Wonder

Kids learn differently outside.

Not a paved trail for twenty minutes, but actual time in it — hands dirty, staying still long enough to see something, learning to name what they find. That's what our programming is built around, for kids especially, and for the adults who come with them.

Our programs Field trips Educator partnerships
Still taking shape Where this is headed

Our programs are still in development, and we're not yet sure how far we'll go into formal education. The primary focus is fostering connection and wonder — especially for kids, but for adults too. Over time, this could grow into a holistic learning system: less a fixed curriculum, more a set of ways for anyone to connect with, and be present on, the land.

Our Programs

Nature exploration — coed, all ages

Our core programming brings youth groups, schools, and families to Beall Heritage Reserve for hands-on time in the field, led by people who actually know the land and enjoy teaching what they've learned.

In the field

  • Native plant and pollinator identification
  • Bird and animal ID in the field
  • Natural movement through prairie and woodland habitat
  • Seasonal walks and habitat exploration

Mindfulness & sit spots

Slowing down is its own skill. Our mindfulness programming uses sit spots and guided observation to help young people practice stillness, presence, and attention in nature — as a standalone class, or woven into other programming.

In development Boys' Nature Skills

A dedicated program focused on traditional outdoor skills: hunting, survival, field navigation, and the kind of nature knowledge that used to get passed down automatically — plant ID, animal tracking, local history, reading the land. We're in the early stages of shaping this. If it sounds like something your son or a group you work with would benefit from, we'd like to hear from you.

Night Hikes

Glow of the Prairie

One of our first community events is a guided night hike at the Reserve. The prairie and woodland at night are a completely different experience — sound, movement, the insects and animals that only come out after dark. Night hikes are on the calendar as an early fundraiser.

Who we work with

Schools, homeschool cooperatives, youth organizations, and family groups from Porter County and surrounding areas. We also offer custom programming for groups with specific interests or age ranges — tell us what you're looking for.

Field Trips & School Programs

Bring your class to the Reserve

A hands-on visit for schools and youth groups, covering native plant and pollinator identification, bird and animal ID, and seasonal habitat exploration — scaled to the age group and time you have.

Booking mechanism & standards alignment in progress We're finalizing how field trips get booked and how sessions map to education standards. In the meantime, reach out directly to start a conversation about your group.

Educator Partnerships

Bring your class. Co-teach with us.

We partner with teachers and program leads who want their students to spend real time on the land, whether that's a single field trip, a recurring co-taught session, or a custom program shaped around your group's goals.
